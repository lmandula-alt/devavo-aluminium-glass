Questions

Frequently asked

Is a frameless glass balustrade safe for a home with children?

Yes, when it uses toughened safety glass of the right thickness and is fixed properly. We specify the glass and fixings for the height and use of each staircase or balcony.

Can you add a glass balustrade to an existing staircase?

Usually, yes. We check the stair structure and choose a fixing method, side-fixed or channel, that suits it.

Do you make trellis gates to fit odd-sized openings?

Yes. Our trellis and security gates are made to measure for each door, window or shopfront.
