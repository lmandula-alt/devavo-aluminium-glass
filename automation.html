Questions

Frequently asked

Can you automate the gate or garage door I already have?

Usually, yes. We check the gate or door, its track and its weight, and recommend a motor that suits it. If the gate needs repairs first, we will tell you.

Do you make the gate and fit the motor yourselves?

Yes. Our workshop builds the gate or garage door, and our team installs it and sets up the motor, remotes and safety beams.

Which gate motors do you install?

We install established South African gate motor brands, and choose the model for the gate's weight, length and daily use. The quote names the motor so you can compare.

Do you do commercial and public-sector gates?

Yes. We have installed heavy-duty gate motors for EC Public Works in Graaff-Reinet and gate automation for EC Social Development.
