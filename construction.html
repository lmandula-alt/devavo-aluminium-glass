Building, renovations and maintenance

Since 2015 Devavo has built, renovated and maintained homes, accommodation, offices and public buildings. Because we also make our own doors, windows and gates, a renovation can be handled by one contractor from foundation to final fitting.

03.1

Renovations & extensions

Home and office renovations, room additions and open-plan conversions, from brickwork and roofing to the final coat of paint.

Why Devavo

A builder that also makes the doors and windows

On most renovations the aluminium is subcontracted, and the programme waits on someone else's workshop. We build the walls and make the frames that go into them, so openings are sized right and the job keeps moving.

  • Brickwork, plastering, ceilings and painting in-house
  • Aluminium, glazing and gates from our own workshop
  • Experience with main contractors such as WBHO
  • Public-sector, commercial and residential projects

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take on small renovations as well as large projects?

Yes. We do everything from a single bathroom or room addition to full refurbishments and masonry packages for main contractors.

Can you refurbish rooms in a hotel or guest house while it stays open?

Yes. We plan the work in phases around occupancy, so rooms come out of service in batches rather than all at once.

Do you work for municipalities and government departments?

Yes. Recent work includes community halls for Nelson Mandela Bay Municipality, building repairs for EC Public Works and remedial works for NHFC.

Are you registered with the CIDB?

Yes. Devavo Trading Enterprise CC is registered with the Construction Industry Development Board. Ask us for our current grading and documents.
