# Sa2ta terms of use

**Last updated:** 8 November 2026

These terms apply when you use the Sa2ta app, run by **Devavo Trading Enterprise CC**
(registration number 2010/087431/23), 5 Diesel Street, North End, Gqeberha, 6001 ("Devavo", "we"). By creating an account you agree to them.

## 1. What Sa2ta is

Sa2ta is a platform that connects people who need a vehicle towed or goods moved ("customers") with independent owners
of bakkies, 4x4s and tow trucks ("helpers"). Helpers are **not** Devavo's employees or agents: each job is an agreement
between the customer and the helper. Devavo provides the app, checks helpers before they can take jobs, and charges
fees as set out below.

## 2. Accounts

- You must be **18 or older** and give true information. One account per person.
- Keep your phone and SMS codes to yourself; you're responsible for what's done with your account.
- Helpers must register their vehicle with true details and photos, keep their licence disc valid, and keep their
  vehicle roadworthy and legal for the work they accept.
- Helpers must hold a **valid driver's licence** for the vehicle (and any trailer) they drive on a job, plus any other
  permit the law requires for the work, and must show it to Devavo when asked.

## 3. How a job works

1. The customer describes the job, adds a photo and sets an offer. The app shows an estimated fair price.
2. Helpers nearby can accept the offer or make a counter-offer. The customer chooses one.
3. When the helper arrives, the customer checks the number plate and the helper's face, and only then gives the
   helper their 4-digit PIN. The job starts with the PIN.
4. The customer pays the helper the agreed price plus the booking fee, by cash, card or EFT as they agree.

## 4. Fees

- **Booking fee:** R10 per job, shown to the customer before they request help, and added to what they pay the helper.
- **Commission:** helpers owe Devavo 15% of each job's price plus the booking fee, recorded in the app when a job is
  finished. Helpers pay it in the app (by card, through Paystack) or by EFT.
- A helper who owes more than R500 can't make new offers until they pay.
- Devavo may change fees with notice in the app; fees on a job are those shown when it was requested.

## 5. Safety and conduct

- Follow the safety guidance shown in the app for each method (rope, tow bar, trailer, carrying). Rope tows are for
  short distances at low speed, with someone steering the towed vehicle.
- Don't accept or request jobs that are unsafe or illegal, or beyond what your vehicle is rated to tow or carry.
- Treat each other with respect. No harassment, threats, discrimination, fraud or theft.
- If the helper doesn't match their photo or number plate, don't give your PIN; report it in the app.

## 7. Insurance

- **Jobs are not insured by Devavo**, and helpers are **not required** to have insurance for towing or for goods in

- Customers who want cover should check their own vehicle or household insurance before accepting an offer.
- The app says this to the customer before they accept a helper's offer.

## 8. Ratings, cancellations and suspension

- Either side can cancel before the job starts. There is **no cancellation fee**.
- Devavo may suspend or close accounts that break these terms, give false information, owe unpaid commission, or put
