# Sa2ta privacy policy

**Last updated:** 8 November 2026

Sa2ta is an app that connects people who need a vehicle towed or goods moved ("customers") with verified owners of
bakkies, 4x4s and tow trucks ("helpers"). It is run by **Devavo Trading Enterprise CC**
(registration number 2010/087431/23), 5 Diesel Street, North End, Gqeberha, 6001, South Africa ("Devavo", "we").

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and your rights under
the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

**Information Officer:** Luthando Mandula, support@devavo.co.za, 072 242 3699

## 1. What we collect

| Information | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile number** | When you sign up or log in | To sign you in with an SMS code and identify your account |
| **Name and profile photo (selfie)** | When you create your account | So customers and helpers can recognise each other at the roadside |
| **Location** (precise) | Only while the app is open | To set the pickup and drop-off, find helpers near you, work out distance and price, and (for helpers on a job) show your position to that job's customer |
| **Job details and photos** | When you ask for help | The vehicle or goods, what's wrong, notes on how to find you, and photos of the vehicle, goods and surroundings, so the helper can find you and see the job |
| **Helper vehicle details and documents** | When you register as a helper | Vehicle type, number plate, make, model, colour, capacity, licence disc expiry, and photos of the vehicle, licence disc and your face, so we can check you before you take jobs |
| **Offers, ratings and job history** | While you use the app | To run the service: offers, accepted jobs, arrival photo, ratings, and what each helper owes Devavo |
| **Commission payments** | When a helper pays Devavo | Amount, date and reference. Card payments are handled by **Paystack**: we never see or store card numbers, only a token from Paystack, the card type and the last 4 digits, so you can pay again with one tap |
| **Notification token** | If you allow notifications | To send you job alerts and updates |

We do **not** collect your email address, contacts, messages, files or browsing history, and we do **not** track your
location when the app is closed.

## 2. Who sees your information

- **The other person on a job.** A customer and the helper on their job see each other's name and photo. The customer
  sees the helper's vehicle, number plate, rating and, while they're on the way, their live position. The helper sees
  the job details, photos, notes and pickup location. A helper's live position is deleted when the job ends.
- **Helpers nearby** see open requests (job details, photos, approximate distance and price) so they can make an offer.
- **Devavo staff** see helpers' vehicle documents to approve them, and commission records to collect what's owed.
- **Service providers** who run parts of Sa2ta for us, only for that purpose:
  Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), Twilio (SMS codes), Google Firebase (notifications),
  MapTiler and openrouteservice (maps, address search and road routes), Paystack (card payments).
  Some of them store data outside South Africa; we use providers bound by agreements that protect it, as POPIA requires.
- **The law.** We may share information if the law requires it, e.g. a court order or a police investigation.

We do **not** sell your personal information and do **not** use it for advertising.

## 3. How long we keep it

- Your account, photos and job history: until you delete your account.
- A helper's live position on a job: deleted as soon as the job is finished or cancelled.
- Commission records (amounts owed and paid): kept after an account is deleted, without the link to the person, for
  Devavo's financial and tax records, for as long as the law requires (5 years).

## 4. Keeping it safe

Everything between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each person can only see
what they're allowed to (for example, only the customer of a job can see their PIN). Card details stay with Paystack.

## 5. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, and you may
object to how we use it. To delete your account, use **Profile → Your account → Delete my account** in the app, or see
our account-deletion page: https://devavo.co.za/sa2ta/delete-account. For anything else, contact our Information Officer above.

If you're not happy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the **Information Regulator**
(https://inforegulator.org.za).

## 6. Children

Sa2ta is for adults (18 and over) only.

## 7. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
