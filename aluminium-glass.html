Questions

Frequently asked

Do you measure on site before quoting?

Yes. We measure every opening ourselves before fabrication, so the frame is made for your wall and not a standard size that needs packing out.

Can you replace my old wooden or steel windows with aluminium?

Yes. Swapping timber and steel frames for aluminium is a large part of our residential work. We remove the old frames, fit the new ones and make good around the opening.

Which colours and finishes are available?

Most of our work is in bronze, charcoal, black, white and natural silver, in powder-coated or anodised finishes that suit the Eastern Cape's salt air. Ask us if you need a specific colour.

Do you do commercial shopfronts and tenders?

Yes. We have installed shopfronts, partitions and replacement glazing for SANParks, South Point, Nelson Mandela University, hospitals and municipal buildings.
