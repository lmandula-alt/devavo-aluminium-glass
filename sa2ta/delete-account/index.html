Sa2ta app

Delete your Sa2ta account

Sa2ta is made by Devavo Trading Enterprise CC.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sa2ta and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom right).
  3. Under Your account, tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@devavo.co.za from any address with the subject "Delete my Sa2ta account" and the mobile number you signed up with. We'll confirm by SMS that it's your number, and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number and photo
  • Your helper vehicle details and documents (photos of the vehicle and licence disc), if you registered as a helper
  • Your requests and jobs, with their photos, notes and locations
  • Your saved card token (card payments), if you saved a card

What we keep

  • Commission records (what a helper owed Devavo and paid), without your name or number, for Devavo's financial and tax records, for as long as the law requires (5 years).
  • Records we must keep because of a legal claim or an investigation, only for as long as needed.
