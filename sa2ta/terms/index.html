Sa2ta terms of use

Last updated: 8 November 2026

These terms apply when you use the Sa2ta app, run by Devavo Trading Enterprise CC (registration number 2010/087431/23), 5 Diesel Street, North End, Gqeberha, 6001 ("Devavo", "we"). By creating an account you agree to them.

1. What Sa2ta is

Sa2ta is a platform that connects people who need a vehicle towed or goods moved ("customers") with independent owners of bakkies, 4x4s and tow trucks ("helpers"). Helpers are not Devavo's employees or agents: each job is an agreement between the customer and the helper. Devavo provides the app, checks helpers before they can take jobs, and charges fees as set out below.

2. Accounts

  • You must be 18 or older and give true information. One account per person.
  • Keep your phone and SMS codes to yourself; you're responsible for what's done with your account.
  • Helpers must register their vehicle with true details and photos, keep their licence disc valid, and keep their vehicle roadworthy and legal for the work they accept.
  • Helpers must hold a valid driver's licence for the vehicle (and any trailer) they drive on a job, plus any other permit the law requires for the work, and must show it to Devavo when asked.

3. How a job works

  1. The customer describes the job, adds a photo and sets an offer. The app shows an estimated fair price.
  2. Helpers nearby can accept the offer or make a counter-offer. The customer chooses one.
  3. When the helper arrives, the customer checks the number plate and the helper's face, and only then gives the helper their 4-digit PIN. The job starts with the PIN.
  4. The customer pays the helper the agreed price plus the booking fee, by cash, card or EFT as they agree.

4. Fees

  • Booking fee: R10 per job, shown to the customer before they request help, and added to what they pay the helper.
  • Commission: helpers owe Devavo 15% of each job's price plus the booking fee, recorded in the app when a job is finished. Helpers pay it in the app (by card, through Paystack) or by EFT.
  • A helper who owes more than R500 can't make new offers until they pay.
  • Devavo may change fees with notice in the app; fees on a job are those shown when it was requested.

5. Safety and conduct

  • Follow the safety guidance shown in the app for each method (rope, tow bar, trailer, carrying). Rope tows are for short distances at low speed, with someone steering the towed vehicle.
  • Don't accept or request jobs that are unsafe or illegal, or beyond what your vehicle is rated to tow or carry.
  • Treat each other with respect. No harassment, threats, discrimination, fraud or theft.
  • If the helper doesn't match their photo or number plate, don't give your PIN; report it in the app.

6. Responsibility for the job

  • Sa2ta only puts customers and helpers in touch. Each job is an agreement between the customer and the helper; Devavo is not a party to it and doesn't tow, carry or store anything itself.
  • The helper is responsible for doing the job with reasonable care and skill, and for their own vehicle.
  • Devavo checks helpers' documents and photos, but cannot guarantee any helper's work, conduct or availability.
  • Devavo is not liable for any loss, damage, injury or cost arising from a job or from using Sa2ta, including damage to or loss of vehicles or goods, delays, and the conduct of a customer or helper. You use Sa2ta and accept jobs at your own risk. This applies to the fullest extent the law allows; nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act that can't be taken away.
  • Disputes about a job should first be raised between the customer and helper; Devavo will help where it reasonably can: support@devavo.co.za.

7. Insurance

  • Jobs are not insured by Devavo, and helpers are not required to have insurance for towing or for goods in transit.
  • Customers who want cover should check their own vehicle or household insurance before accepting an offer.
  • The app says this to the customer before they accept a helper's offer.

8. Ratings, cancellations and suspension

  • Customers can rate helpers after a job. Ratings must be honest.
  • Either side can cancel before the job starts. There is no cancellation fee.
  • Devavo may suspend or close accounts that break these terms, give false information, owe unpaid commission, or put others at risk.

9. Your information

How we handle personal information is explained in our privacy policy: https://devavo.co.za/sa2ta/privacy.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms; we'll show the new date and tell you in the app about important changes. These terms are governed by the law of South Africa.
